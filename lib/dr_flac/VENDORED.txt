One change to src/dr_flac.h, made 2026-11-16 and marked "midiTracker":
drflac__calculate_prediction_64_s32() returns DRFLAC_PREDICTION_64_S32()
when that is defined. src/dr_flac.c defines it as flacPredict64(), the
same sums from 32-bit products (asm in RAM on the RP2040, C on the host),
exact while samples fit in 25 bits. src/dr_flac_config.h sets the options (no
stdio, Ogg, wchar or SIMD, a 2 KB read buffer, no heap or assert) and is
the header to include; src/dr_flac.c builds the implementation with them.
Memory comes from the allocation callbacks FlacPlayer passes (the shared
RAM arena), see src/flac_file.cpp.
